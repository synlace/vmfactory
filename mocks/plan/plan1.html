4 · interactive replay — the plan assembling: clone → read → the spec derive (one llm call) → grounding → the four per-method calls landing as they finish, each lane row flipping from waiting to plan or blocked the moment its call returns. The lane buttons (or arrow keys) filter the event log; all is the top of the cycle. Lane rows carry state words during the replay; the shipped render collapses them into the section-1 shape.
